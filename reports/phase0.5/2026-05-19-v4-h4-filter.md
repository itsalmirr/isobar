## The H4 filter

The predicate, in plain English:

```
admit a trade IFF:
    series  ≠ KXHIGHNY            (exclude NYC HIGH entirely)
AND (series.startswith("KXLOWT") (i.e. low-temp bracket)
     OR  trade_price_cents ≤ 9    (deep no, OTM short)
     OR  trade_price_cents ≥ 91)  (deep yes, OTM long)
```

Three exclusion / inclusion rules. Each has a defensible physical
hypothesis:

1. **Exclude `KXHIGHNY`** — NYC HIGH alone accounted for **−$4,118 of the −$4,964 total loss** in v3 (83%). NYC has the highest weather variability of the four registered stations (coast + urban heat island + season transitions). GFS is known to be biased on stations like KNYC; ECMWF blend (still on the roadmap) might close some of this gap, but excluding the worst-fit station is the cheapest immediate win.

2. **Include `KXLOWT*`** — low-temp brackets (TMIN). Tomorrow's TMIN happens at dawn and is highly predictable from tonight's running minimum + radiative cooling physics. TMAX happens at midday and depends on cloud cover, sea breezes, convection — fundamentally harder for a daily forecast. Calibration *should* naturally work better on lows; the data shows it does (KXLOWTMIA +14.7¢/trade, KXLOWTLAX +7.1¢, KXLOWTCHI +6.1¢).

3. **Include extreme-OTM bands (price ≤ 9¢ or ≥ 91¢)** — the market underprices low-probability outcomes (well-documented behavioural pattern; e.g., favorites-longshot bias). Even with a poor calibrator, paying 5¢ to win 95¢ on a 7% probability is positive EV. Our v3 data confirms: trades at price ≤ 5¢ have 5.8% win rate but +2.28¢/trade avg PnL.

## What this is, and what this is NOT

**This IS:**
- The first positive-EV strategy variant we've measured on real Kalshi tape with NWS CLI truth grounding.
- A combination of three hypothesis-driven exclusions, each defensible from physics or market microstructure.
- A reproducible code path: `--entry-filter h4` in `bin/edge-scan`.
- Roughly $130/day at notional scale on these 5 days; ~$5-$15/day at the proposed $200-bankroll Phase 1 caps ($10/ticker, $25/series, $50/portfolio).

**This is NOT:**
- A proven trading strategy. **5 days of leave-one-out CV is a hypothesis test, not evidence.** Single-day swings ranged from −$60 to +$583.
- Statistically distinguishable from chance at any reasonable confidence level. With n=5 days, a daily Sharpe of 0.68 has a 95% CI roughly `[-1.2, +2.6]` — wide enough to include "no edge".
- Free of overfitting risk. The H4 conditions were discovered by examining which subsets of the same 6-day window were positive; the leave-one-out CV is a weak guard against this, not a proof.

## Forward-validation plan

The only way to know if H4 is real is to score it on data that did not exist when we discovered it. We have it.

- **Forward tape**: the recorder has been running on a fresh Vultr session since 2026-05-19 02:45 UTC, capturing 25-50 frames/sec from 96 active markets. The 2026-05-19 onwards tape is fully out-of-sample for the H4 filter.
- **Forward truth**: NWS CLI bulletins are issued the morning after the civil date they resolve. To backtest H4 on bracket valid_date D, we need CLI for D+1 (issued AM of D+1) — which takes ~24 hours of latency from the bracket's settlement.
- **Recommended window**: accumulate ≥ 10 trading days of forward tape before the first re-test (≥ 50,000 entered trades expected at H4 throughput). Then `make backtest TAPE=./data START=2026-05-19 END=2026-05-29` reproduces the verdict end-to-end.

**v4 → v5 gate criteria** (proposed):
- ≥ 10 forward trading days, n ≥ 25,000 H4 entries
- Aggregate PnL > 0
- Daily Sharpe ≥ 0.5
- Positive days ≥ 60% (6/10)
- Maximum single-day drawdown < 1× total positive-day PnL (i.e. one bad day cannot erase the trend)

If H4 passes v5: **unblock Phase 1 paper bot with H4 as the signal**.
If H4 fails v5: **file ADR-0014** documenting the v3 calibration thesis as a failed-trading-gate; project becomes the forecasting-research artifact the audit proposed.
